# Content Creator Pack

For creators and social teams. Your Bot drafts threads, scripts, memes, and visuals in
your voice — everything parked for you to publish.

## Skills

- [Thread Writer](../skills/creative-content/thread-writer/SKILL.md) — topic/doc → structured, on-voice X thread.
- [Short Video Script Writer](../skills/creative-content/short-video-script-writer/SKILL.md) — shoot-ready Reels/Shorts scripts.
- [Image Concept Generator](../skills/creative-content/image-concept-generator/SKILL.md) — on-brand concepts + generation prompts.
- [Meme Maker](../skills/creative-content/meme-maker/SKILL.md) — topical, brand-safe memes.
- [Hook & Headline Tester](../skills/creative-content/hook-and-headline-tester/SKILL.md) — ranks openers against your goal.
- [Brand Voice Keeper](../skills/creative-content/brand-voice-keeper/SKILL.md) — rewrites/audits copy to your voice.
- [Content Repurposer](../skills/creative-content/content-repurposer/SKILL.md) — one asset → many channel-native pieces.

## Suggested cadence

- **On idea / noteworthy event:** Thread Writer, Short Video Script Writer, Meme Maker.
- **Before publish:** Hook & Headline Tester, Brand Voice Keeper.
- **On new cornerstone asset:** Content Repurposer; Image Concept Generator as needed.
