# Founder Pack

For solo founders and execs who need leverage across the whole company. Wire these up
and your Bot keeps you oriented, on top of the inbox, and ahead of the day.

## Skills

- [Chief of Staff](../skills/general/chief-of-staff/SKILL.md) — one prioritized read-out across Slack, email, calendar, and notes.
- [Daily Briefing Writer](../skills/general/daily-briefing-writer/SKILL.md) — signal-only news brief each morning.
- [Inbox Manager](../skills/general/inbox-manager/SKILL.md) — triage + drafted replies, approval-gated.
- [Status Report Writer](../skills/general/status-report-writer/SKILL.md) — one living action list and a morning digest.
- [Meeting Prep Buddy](../skills/sales/meeting-prep-buddy/SKILL.md) — prep pack before every external meeting.
- [Deep Research Brief](../skills/research-intelligence/deep-research-brief/SKILL.md) — decision-ready research on demand.
- [Travel Coordinator](../skills/life-leverage/travel-coordinator/SKILL.md) — trips booked to your rules after you confirm.

## Suggested cadence

- **Morning (7:00–8:00):** Chief of Staff → Daily Briefing → Status Report.
- **Through the day:** Inbox Manager every couple of hours; Meeting Prep before each meeting.
- **On demand:** Deep Research Brief, Travel Coordinator.
