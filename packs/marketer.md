# Marketer Pack

For growth and content marketers. Your Bot watches the landscape, drafts on-voice
content, keeps campaigns wired, and reports what's working.

## Skills

- [Social Media Manager](../skills/marketing/social-media-manager/SKILL.md) — on-voice drafts when something ships.
- [Newsletter Writer](../skills/marketing/newsletter-writer/SKILL.md) — the recurring issue from what actually happened.
- [Competitive Intelligence Analyst](../skills/marketing/competitive-intelligence-analyst/SKILL.md) — only material competitor shifts.
- [SEO / AEO Auditor](../skills/marketing/seo-aeo-auditor/SKILL.md) — search + answer-engine movement and a fix plan.
- [Paid Media](../skills/marketing/paid-media/SKILL.md) — budget reallocation recommendations, approval-gated.
- [Content Repurposer](../skills/creative-content/content-repurposer/SKILL.md) — one asset → a channel-native pack.
- [Trend Radar](../skills/research-intelligence/trend-radar/SKILL.md) — real-time emerging trends in your space.

## Suggested cadence

- **Daily:** Social Media Manager queue, Trend Radar, Paid Media check.
- **Nightly:** Competitive Intelligence Analyst.
- **Weekly:** SEO / AEO Auditor.
- **On publish / monthly:** Content Repurposer, Newsletter Writer.
