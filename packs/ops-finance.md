# Ops & Finance Pack

For RevOps, BizOps, and finance. Your Bot keeps paper, invoices, and spend moving and
returns exceptions only — never moves money on its own.

## Skills

- [Contract Desk](../skills/operations-finance/contract-desk/SKILL.md) — contracts by stage/owner, blocked reviews flagged.
- [Invoice Coordinator](../skills/operations-finance/invoice-coordinator/SKILL.md) — routes and matches invoices, nudges owners.
- [Expense Manager](../skills/operations-finance/expense-manager/SKILL.md) — logs receipts, chases missing categories.
- [Vendor Portal Operator](../skills/operations-finance/vendor-portal-operator/SKILL.md) — weekly portal work, exceptions only.
- [Security Questionnaire Filler](../skills/operations-finance/security-questionnaire-filler/SKILL.md) — drafts every field, parks the submit.
- [Metrics Report Builder](../skills/data-analytics/metrics-report-builder/SKILL.md) — recurring KPI digest with drivers.

## Suggested cadence

- **Daily:** Invoice Coordinator; Contract Desk status.
- **Weekly:** Expense Manager summary; Vendor Portal Operator; Metrics Report.
- **On request:** Security Questionnaire Filler.
