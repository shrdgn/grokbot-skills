# Recruiter Pack

For recruiting and people ops. Your Bot sources, screens, schedules, and onboards —
recommendations only, humans decide.

## Skills

- [Talent Scout](../skills/recruiting-people/talent-scout/SKILL.md) — sources + drafts outreach, deduped against the ATS.
- [Hiring Screener](../skills/recruiting-people/hiring-screener/SKILL.md) — scores applications against a defined bar.
- [Calendar Coordinator](../skills/recruiting-people/calendar-coordinator/SKILL.md) — schedules loops and chases the holds.
- [Onboarding Manager](../skills/recruiting-people/onboarding-manager/SKILL.md) — checklist, docs, day-one answers, routing.
- [Deep Research Brief](../skills/research-intelligence/deep-research-brief/SKILL.md) — market/comp research for a role or candidate.

## Suggested cadence

- **Nightly:** Talent Scout sourcing; review outreach each morning.
- **On new applications:** Hiring Screener (daily batch).
- **On request:** Calendar Coordinator; Onboarding Manager kicks off at start date.
