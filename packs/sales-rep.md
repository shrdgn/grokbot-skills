# Sales Rep Pack

For AEs and full-cycle sellers. Your Bot preps meetings, keeps CRM clean, drafts
outbound, and coaches your calls — all approval-gated.

## Skills

- [Meeting Prep Buddy](../skills/sales/meeting-prep-buddy/SKILL.md) — who's in the room, last touch, agenda.
- [Account Research Specialist](../skills/sales/account-research-specialist/SKILL.md) — fit/warmth scoring + research pack.
- [Sales Outbound](../skills/sales/sales-outbound/SKILL.md) — overnight research + personalized drafts to approve.
- [CRM Operations Manager](../skills/sales/crm-operations-manager/SKILL.md) — CRM hygiene before and after meetings.
- [Sales Call Coach](../skills/sales/sales-call-coach/SKILL.md) — timestamped coaching + a score per call.
- [Pipeline Analyst](../skills/sales/pipeline-analyst/SKILL.md) — Monday scoreboard, stalls, and commit risk.

## Suggested cadence

- **Nightly:** Sales Outbound builds the review queue.
- **Around meetings:** Meeting Prep before, CRM Operations after.
- **Monday:** Pipeline Analyst scoreboard.
- **After each call:** Sales Call Coach.
