# Support & Success Pack

For customer success and support teams. Your Bot clears the queue, watches account
health, and keeps every account warm — drafts only, quiet when clean.

## Skills

- [Ticket Triage Specialist](../skills/customer-success-support/ticket-triage-specialist/SKILL.md) — triage + drafted replies, silent when clean.
- [Account Health](../skills/customer-success-support/account-health/SKILL.md) — ranked risk/expansion watch list.
- [Account Manager](../skills/customer-success-support/account-manager/SKILL.md) — call prep + drafted follow-ups.
- [Enablement Fulfillment Specialist](../skills/customer-success-support/enablement-fulfillment-specialist/SKILL.md) — finds assets, drafts the reply with links.
- [Call & Video Summarizer](../skills/research-intelligence/call-and-video-summarizer/SKILL.md) — recording → decisions + action items.

## Suggested cadence

- **Every 30–60 min (support hours):** Ticket Triage Specialist.
- **Weekly:** Account Health watch list.
- **Around calls:** Account Manager prep + follow-up; Call & Video Summarizer after.
- **On request:** Enablement Fulfillment Specialist.
