# Brand Voice Keeper

Codifies your brand voice from real examples, then rewrites or audits any copy against
it — so everything sounds like you, no matter who drafted it.

## Inputs
- Voice examples (best-of copy) and any existing style guide.
- The copy to rewrite or check, and its channel.

## Steps
1. If no voice profile exists, derive one from examples: tone, rhythm, do/don't,
   vocabulary, formality — and save it.
2. For a rewrite: revise the draft to the profile, preserving meaning.
3. For an audit: score the draft against the profile and flag off-voice lines with fixes.
4. Return the result with a short note on what changed and why.

## Decision rules
- Preserve meaning and facts — voice changes wording, not substance.
- Respect channel norms (a tweet ≠ a whitepaper).
- Flag, don't silently alter, any claim or number.

## Definition of done
- A saved voice profile (first run) and either an on-voice rewrite or a scored audit
  with specific line-level fixes.

## Safety & approvals
- Suggests copy; doesn't publish.
- Won't invent facts to fit the voice.

## Suggested routine
- On demand; as a pre-publish check step for any copy.
