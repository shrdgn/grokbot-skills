# Content Repurposer

Takes one source asset and produces a pack of channel-native derivatives in your voice,
so a single piece of work fuels a week of content instead of one post.

## Inputs
- The source asset (blog, talk transcript, webinar, long post).
- Target channels and your voice.

## Steps
1. Extract the core ideas, best quotes, and any data/visuals from the source.
2. Produce channel-native derivatives, e.g.: an X thread, a LinkedIn post, a newsletter
   blurb, a short-video script, and 2–3 quote-card concepts.
3. Adapt tone/length/format to each channel — not copy-paste.
4. Link each derivative back to the source and suggest a posting order.

## Decision rules
- Each derivative stands on its own and fits its channel's norms.
- Preserve the source's facts; mark anything needing confirmation.
- No duplicate cross-posting of identical text.

## Definition of done
- A content pack: one derivative per target channel (plus quote cards), each on-voice
  and channel-native, with a suggested sequence — ready to approve.

## Safety & approvals
- **Drafts only** — you approve/publish each piece.
- Attributes quotes correctly; flags unverified claims.

## Suggested routine
- Triggered when a new cornerstone asset publishes.
