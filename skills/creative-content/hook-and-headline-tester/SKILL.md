# Hook & Headline Tester

Generates a spread of hooks/headlines/subject lines, then ranks them against your goal
and audience with reasons — so you ship the strongest opener, not the first one.

## Inputs
- The asset (post, email, article, ad) and its one goal (opens, clicks, replies).
- Audience and any brand/voice constraints.
- Where it appears (X, email subject, ad headline, blog title).

## Steps
1. Clarify the single goal and the reader's state of mind.
2. Generate 10–15 options across angles (curiosity, benefit, contrarian, specific,
   question, number).
3. Score each on clarity, specificity, curiosity, and goal-fit.
4. Rank and explain the top 3–5; note which to A/B test against each other.

## Decision rules
- Reward specificity over hype; penalize clickbait that the body can't pay off.
- Match channel length limits and voice.
- No fabricated numbers or false promises.

## Definition of done
- A ranked set of options with scores and rationale, a recommended top 3–5, and a
  suggested A/B pairing.

## Safety & approvals
- Suggestions only; publishes nothing.
- Flags any headline the content can't actually deliver on.

## Suggested routine
- On demand, as a step before shipping any high-stakes copy.
