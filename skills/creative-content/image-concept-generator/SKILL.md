# Image Concept Generator

Turns a creative brief into several distinct on-brand image concepts with
generation-ready prompts, so you pick a direction instead of describing pixels.

## Inputs
- The brief: subject, purpose, placement (post/ad/blog/deck), mood, any copy overlay.
- Brand system: colors, style, do/don't references.
- Aspect ratios needed.

## Steps
1. Restate the brief and constraints in one line.
2. Propose 3–5 distinct concepts (different angle/composition/style each).
3. For each, write a detailed, generation-ready prompt with style, composition,
   lighting, palette, and negative prompts.
4. Generate previews; present concepts side by side with the prompt used.
5. On your pick, produce final sizes in the needed ratios.

## Decision rules
- Concepts must be genuinely different, not variations of one idea.
- Stay within brand style; flag when the brief pushes off-brand.
- No real logos/people/trademarks unless supplied and cleared.

## Definition of done
- A concept board (previews + prompts) and, on selection, final images in the required
  aspect ratios.

## Safety & approvals
- Avoids depicting real, identifiable people or protected IP unless provided/authorized.
- Marks AI-generated where your policy requires disclosure.

## Suggested routine
- On demand from a content or campaign brief.
