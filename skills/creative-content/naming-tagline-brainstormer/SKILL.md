# Naming & Tagline Brainstormer

Generates naming and tagline options across distinct directions, then pressure-tests the
shortlist for availability and pitfalls — so you decide from a vetted few.

## Inputs
- What's being named, the positioning, and the audience.
- Constraints (length, tone, must-avoid words, naming family).

## Steps
1. Define 3–4 naming directions (descriptive, evocative, invented, etc.).
2. Generate options within each direction.
3. Cull to a shortlist of 8–12 with a one-line rationale each.
4. Sanity-check the shortlist: domain/handle availability, obvious trademark clashes,
   unintended meanings, pronounceability.
5. Recommend a top 3 with tradeoffs, plus a tagline for each.

## Decision rules
- Shortlist only names that pass the basic checks; flag risky ones.
- Note availability checks are indicative — real trademark clearance needs counsel.
- No offensive/unintended meanings across major languages.

## Definition of done
- A vetted shortlist with rationale, availability/risk notes, and a recommended top 3
  with taglines and tradeoffs.

## Safety & approvals
- Availability/trademark checks are advisory, not legal clearance — says so.

## Suggested routine
- On demand for a launch, feature, or campaign.
