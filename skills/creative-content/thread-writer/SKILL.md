# Thread Writer

Turns a topic or source into a tight, on-voice X thread — a hook that earns the next
tap, one idea per post, and a payoff — ready for you to publish.

## Inputs
- The topic or source material (doc, launch notes, data, a rough take).
- Your voice/handle history and the thread's goal (educate, launch, opinion).
- Target length (default 5–9 posts).

## Steps
1. Extract the single core idea and the takeaway you want readers to leave with.
2. Write 3–5 hook options for post 1; pick the strongest, keep the rest as alts.
3. Outline one idea per post; write each ≤ 280 chars, concrete and skimmable.
4. End with a payoff + optional CTA (follow, link, reply prompt).
5. Suggest where an image/chart strengthens a post.

## Decision rules
- Hook must promise a specific payoff — no vague "let's talk about…".
- One idea per post; cut anything that doesn't earn its place.
- Match your real voice; no engagement-bait or fabricated stats (mark `[CONFIRM]`).

## Definition of done
- A numbered thread draft with alt hooks, per-post text within limits, image
  suggestions, and a payoff — ready to approve and publish.

## Safety & approvals
- **Never posts on its own** — drafts for your review.
- Flags any claim/stat it couldn't source.

## Suggested routine
- On demand, or triggered when something noteworthy ships.
