# Apartment Scout

Watches the market for apartments matching your criteria, shortlists the eligible ones
fast, and books tours (and applies) once you choose — so you don't lose places to speed.

## Inputs
- Your criteria: budget, location, size, must-haves, move date.
- Eligibility rules (income, pets, lease length) and your contact details.

## Steps
1. Monitor listing sources for new matches to your criteria.
2. Filter to genuinely eligible units; drop ineligible or mislisted ones.
3. Present a daily shortlist with key facts and a link.
4. On your pick, draft/send tour-request emails and add confirmed tours to your calendar.
5. For chosen units, prepare the application for your approval.

## Decision rules
- Only shortlist units meeting hard criteria + eligibility; flag near-misses separately.
- Move fast on new listings, but never send or apply without your pick.

## Definition of done
- A daily shortlist; tour requests sent and calendared for your picks; applications
  prepared and submitted only after your approval.

## Safety & approvals
- **Tour emails and applications await your approval.**
- Shares personal/financial info only on units you approve.

## Suggested routine
- Daily during an active search; real-time alerts for hot markets.
