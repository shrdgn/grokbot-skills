# Compelling Events Monitor

Watches your target accounts' and leaders' activity for genuine "compelling events" and
drafts on-voice engagement so you show up when it actually matters.

## Inputs
- Watchlist of accounts, leaders, and handles.
- Trigger types (awards, funding, launches, hiring, milestones).
- Your engagement voice/style.

## Steps
1. Scan the watchlist for new posts/news since last run.
2. Filter to real compelling events; drop routine chatter.
3. For each, draft a comment or quote-repost in your voice that adds value.
4. Deliver a short digest ranked by relevance.

## Decision rules
- Only surface events worth engaging on; quality over volume.
- Drafts add a genuine point of view — no empty "Congrats!".
- Skip anything politically or reputationally risky; flag instead.

## Definition of done
- A ranked digest of engage-worthy posts, each with a drafted, on-voice response ready
  to approve.

## Safety & approvals
- **Nothing posts automatically** — you approve each engagement.
- Avoids sensitive/controversial topics.

## Suggested routine
- Every morning; optional midday check for fast movers.
