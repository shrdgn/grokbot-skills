# Newsletter Writer

Drafts the recurring newsletter from what actually happened — launches, wins, upcoming
events — in your voice, ready for a single editing pass before send.

## Inputs
- Sources of "what's new": launches, changelog, wins, calendar.
- Newsletter structure/template and your voice.
- Send date and audience.

## Steps
1. Gather updates since the last issue; pick the strongest items.
2. Draft the issue to the template: intro, sections, CTAs, subject-line options.
3. Write in your voice; keep each item tight with a link.
4. Park the draft for review with a note on what needs confirmation.

## Decision rules
- Lead with the highest-impact update; cut filler.
- No unverified claims/metrics — mark `[CONFIRM]`.
- Include clear CTAs and links for each item.

## Definition of done
- A complete draft issue in the template with subject-line options, ready for one
  editing pass, plus a list of items to confirm.

## Safety & approvals
- **Never sends** — parks for marketing to edit and send.
- Flags anything needing legal/PR review.

## Suggested routine
- Monthly, delivered a few days before the scheduled send.
