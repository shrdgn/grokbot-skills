# SEO / AEO Auditor

Monitors search and answer-engine visibility, flags technical issues, and returns a
prioritized optimization plan — so you know if you're winning or losing and what to fix.

## Inputs
- Your site + target keywords/topics.
- Competitor domains.
- Answer-engine prompts to track (AEO) and your technical baseline.

## Steps
1. Pull rankings, impressions, and clicks; compute week-over-week movement.
2. Check answer-engine visibility for target prompts (are you cited?).
3. Crawl for technical issues (broken links, slow pages, indexing, metadata gaps).
4. Compare movement vs. competitors on shared terms.
5. Return a prioritized optimization plan.

## Decision rules
- Prioritize fixes by traffic/visibility impact × effort.
- Separate ranking movement from technical issues from content gaps.
- Cite the data behind each "gaining/losing" claim.

## Definition of done
- A report: visibility trend (search + AEO), competitor deltas, ranked technical issues,
  and an actionable optimization plan with priorities.

## Safety & approvals
- Audits and recommends; makes no site changes.

## Suggested routine
- Weekly movement check; monthly deep technical + content audit.
