# Talent Scout

Keeps the top of the recruiting funnel full — sourcing, deduping against the ATS, and
drafting personalized outreach in your voice — then schedules once you approve.

## Inputs
- Role profile / ideal-candidate criteria.
- Sourcing channels and your outreach voice.
- ATS (to avoid re-contacting existing candidates).

## Steps
1. Source candidates matching the profile.
2. Dedupe against the ATS; drop anyone already in process or recently contacted.
3. Score and rank by fit with rationale.
4. Draft personalized outreach in your voice per candidate.
5. Queue for approval; on approval, send and handle scheduling of responders.

## Decision rules
- Match the role criteria; cite the fit rationale per candidate.
- Never re-contact existing/active candidates.
- Personalization must be specific and true; respect consent rules.

## Definition of done
- A ranked candidate list with drafted outreach, deduped against the ATS, ready for
  approval; scheduling handled for those who reply.

## Safety & approvals
- **Outreach sends only after your approval.** Drafts and queues by default.
- Honors consent/privacy; no gated-data scraping.

## Suggested routine
- Sources overnight; you review and approve outreach each morning.
