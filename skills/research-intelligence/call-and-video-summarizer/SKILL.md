# Call & Video Summarizer

Turns a call, meeting, or long video into a structured summary — key points, decisions,
owners, and timestamped highlights — so nobody has to rewatch to catch up.

## Inputs
- The recording/transcript or video link.
- What you care about (decisions, action items, a specific topic).

## Steps
1. Ingest the transcript/recording.
2. Extract: TL;DR, key points, decisions made, action items (owner + due where stated).
3. Pull 3–6 timestamped highlights linking to the moment.
4. Note open questions and any follow-ups.
5. Deliver to your chosen destination.

## Decision rules
- Attribute decisions/actions to who actually said them; no invented commitments.
- Keep the TL;DR to a few lines; detail below.
- Timestamps must point to the real moment.

## Definition of done
- A summary with TL;DR, key points, decisions, owner-tagged action items, timestamped
  highlights, and open questions.

## Safety & approvals
- Summarizes only; sends/assigns nothing on its own.
- Flags anything ambiguous rather than guessing intent.

## Suggested routine
- Automatically after recordings, or on demand for a video/transcript.
