# Deep Research Brief

Turns a research question into a structured, decision-ready brief with citations — the
synthesis you'd want from an analyst, not a pile of tabs.

## Inputs
- The question and why it matters (the decision it feeds).
- Scope, depth, and any must-cover angles or sources to trust/avoid.

## Steps
1. Break the question into sub-questions.
2. Gather evidence across sources; prefer primary and recent.
3. Triangulate — corroborate key claims across independent sources; note disagreement.
4. Synthesize into: answer up top, then findings by sub-question, then open questions.
5. Cite every material claim with source + date; flag confidence.

## Decision rules
- Lead with the answer/BLUF, then support it.
- Every claim is sourced; separate fact from inference; note uncertainty honestly.
- Prefer primary sources; discount low-quality or single-source claims.

## Definition of done
- A brief: bottom line up front, findings by sub-question with citations, a confidence
  note, and open questions — decision-ready.

## Safety & approvals
- Never fabricates sources, quotes, or figures; says "not found" when it isn't.
- Marks low-confidence or contested claims explicitly.

## Suggested routine
- On demand for a real decision or question.
