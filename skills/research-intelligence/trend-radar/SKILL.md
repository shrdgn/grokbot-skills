# Trend Radar

Scans X and the web in real time for emerging trends and narratives in your space and
delivers a ranked digest — so you spot momentum early, with the receipts.

## Inputs
- Topics, keywords, hashtags, and accounts to watch.
- Your space/context (to judge relevance) and how far back to look.

## Steps
1. Scan the watchlist for rising conversations, posts, and coverage since last run.
2. Cluster into distinct trends/narratives; estimate momentum (velocity, reach).
3. For each, capture what it is, who's driving it, why it matters to you, and a source.
4. Rank by relevance × momentum; flag anything you should react to now.

## Decision rules
- Surface genuinely emerging trends, not steady background noise.
- Distinguish organic momentum from a single viral post or manufactured push.
- Cite representative sources; separate observation from speculation.

## Definition of done
- A ranked digest of emerging trends, each with a summary, driver, why-it-matters, and
  source links — plus any react-now flags.

## Safety & approvals
- Reports only; posts and engages nothing.
- Notes when a "trend" is thin or unverified.

## Suggested routine
- Daily radar; on-demand deep scan around a launch or event.
