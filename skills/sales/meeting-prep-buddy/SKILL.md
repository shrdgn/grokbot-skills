# Meeting Prep Buddy

Delivers a prep pack before each external meeting so you know who's attending, the
history, what's open, and what to drive toward.

## Inputs
- Calendar (detects meetings + attendees).
- CRM, call history (Gong), email, and Slack for context.

## Steps
1. For each upcoming external meeting, identify attendees and their roles.
2. Pull account/opportunity status, last touch, and recent calls/emails.
3. List open threads, commitments, and unanswered questions.
4. Draft a suggested agenda and 2–3 goals for the meeting.
5. Deliver the pack ahead of time.

## Decision rules
- Prioritize the most recent and decision-relevant context.
- Flag risks (stalled deal, unhappy signals) and any promise owed to them.
- Keep it skimmable — one screen where possible.

## Definition of done
- A prep pack per meeting: attendees + roles, account status, last touch, open items,
  suggested agenda and goals, delivered before the meeting.

## Safety & approvals
- Read-only; doesn't message attendees or alter records.

## Suggested routine
- Nightly for the next day, plus a refresh ~30 minutes before each meeting.
