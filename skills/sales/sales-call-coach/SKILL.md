# Sales Call Coach

Reviews recorded calls and returns specific, timestamped coaching plus a score against
your methodology, so reps improve call over call.

## Inputs
- Recorded call (Gong) and its transcript.
- Your sales methodology and coaching rubric (discovery, objection handling, next steps,
  talk ratio, executive presence).

## Steps
1. Analyze the transcript + timeline against the rubric.
2. Identify strong moments and misses, each with a timestamp and a quote.
3. Score each rubric dimension with rationale.
4. Write 2–3 concrete "homework" actions for next time.
5. Deliver the coaching card privately to the rep.

## Decision rules
- Every point cites a timestamp/quote — no generic advice.
- Balance strengths and gaps; prioritize the highest-leverage fix.
- Coach behavior, not personality.

## Definition of done
- A coaching card per call: dimension scores with rationale, timestamped strengths and
  misses, and 2–3 specific next-call actions.

## Safety & approvals
- Private to the rep (and their manager if configured); not shared broadly.
- Analysis only; no action taken on the deal.

## Suggested routine
- Automatically after each recorded external call.
